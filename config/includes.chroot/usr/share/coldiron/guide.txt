COLDIRON — First-time guide (plain language)
---------------------------------------------
What is COLDIRON?  A computer with one job: keep your bitcoin
safe, offline. It never connects to the internet.

What is a seed?    Your 12 or 24 words. They ARE your bitcoin.
Anyone with the words controls the money. Keep them private:
written on paper, never photographed.

What is a passphrase?  An optional extra secret ("25th word")
that works WITH your words. Without it the words alone cannot
spend. Never stored anywhere — keep it on paper, apart from
the words.

What is a vault?   An encrypted folder on your USB drive.
Your seed backups live inside it, locked with a passphrase.

What is an address? Where people send you bitcoin. Safe to
share. Sparrow makes it from your seed — never the reverse.

Your first time — do these in order:
  1. Option 1 — roll dice, write down the words. Check them twice.
  2. Option 3 — back the words up, encrypted, on the vault USB.
  3. Option 5 — open Sparrow, type the words, see your address.
  4. Option 8 — run the security check, confirm it all passes.

The rules (all matter):
  • Never connect to the internet.
  • Never photograph or type the words on any online device.
  • Use fair dice (casino-grade, sharp corners).
  • Paper is the primary backup; the vault copy is the spare.
  • A passphrase lives on paper only — forget it, lose it.
